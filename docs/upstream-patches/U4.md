# Replace full-text sources atomically

Replacing an article's full text was done field by field: adopting a JATS text kept the previous
PDF in `article_files`, a text without a methods section inherited the previous source's
`methods_text`, and an error in the middle (caught by the batch task, which then commits its
progress) could leave a half-replaced source.

- `_apply_fulltext()` does the whole replacement (sections, source, url, note, PDF stored or
  deleted, methods from this text only) inside `session.begin_nested()`; the caller still commits.
- `apply_uploaded_pdf()` and `complete_article()` both go through it.
- Batch task: read `pmid` before the attempt (a savepoint rollback may expire the ORM object).
- Tests: JATS/PDF × with/without methods replacement, upload, rollback even when the caller
  commits, batch continuing after one failed write.

Tested: `ruff check`, `ruff format --check`, `pytest tests/test_fulltext.py tests/test_tasks_import.py tests/test_annotations_api.py` (64 passed); 6 of the new cases fail on `main`.
